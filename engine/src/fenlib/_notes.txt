We can still optimise move generation:

 - Branchless programming
 - precomputation (Magic bitboards)
 - Compiler optimizations

We can also optimise other functions:

in_check: Currently, we check the opponent attack pattern against the king position.
We are doing too much work here since we check the opponent king's attacks as well.
Instead, for pawns and knights, we can take the king, compute its attacks as if it was a pawn or knight,
then check that against the pawn/knight bitboard. This is better than checking the attack of each piece.
For sliding pieces, we can do something similar.

is_legal_move, is_valid_board and get_zobrist can also be optimised.
